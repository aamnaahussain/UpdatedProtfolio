KPI Tracking & Performance Management System

The Business Development department relied on manual Excel sheets to track KPIs. As the team grew, the sheets became unwieldy, data was inconsistent, and there was no reliable historical record, which made performance evaluation and workforce decisions difficult for both BD leadership and HR.

My Role
  • Identified the problem and proposed the solution, recognizing that manual tracking was limiting transparency and reliable performance evaluation
  • Led the initiative end-to-end, from ideation through deployment, owning every stage independently
  • Ran requirements gathering across BD leadership, the BD team, and HR to understand what each group needed to track and why
  • Defined the scope and data structure for each KPI, prioritizing metrics most relevant to monthly performance evaluation and reward decisions
  • Owned the design direction, coordinating with UI/UX designers and engineering to align the interface with real stakeholder workflows
  • Managed the development timeline and conducted initial system testing / UAT before handoff to the BD team for final validation
Outcome
  • Fully adopted across the entire BD department, and extended beyond its original scope to evaluate interns and new hires during probation
  • Replaced a fragmented, error-prone manual process with a transparent, dashboard-based system used by HR and senior leadership for decision-making
  • Significantly reduced the time and effort previously spent maintaining Excel trackers, freeing up team capacity for higher-value work
